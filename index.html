Who We Are

End-to-end manufacturing for brands that want to launch faster

Himherbal Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted private label and contract manufacturing partner for beauty, personal care, fragrance, herbal and healthcare products. We help brand owners, startups, distributors, exporters and retail chains bring high-quality products to market — without the overhead of running a factory.

From concept and formulation to production, packaging and delivery, our integrated facility and in-house R&D team handle the entire journey under one roof, backed by internationally recognised quality standards.

  • Custom & ready formulations across 12+ categories
  • Low MOQs for startups, scalable capacity for enterprises
  • Complete regulatory & export documentation support
