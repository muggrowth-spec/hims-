Who We Are

A legacy built on quality, trust & craftsmanship

Himherbal Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. began with a simple belief — that world-class products shouldn't require every brand to own a factory. What started as a small herbal formulation lab has grown into a full-scale contract and private label manufacturer serving hundreds of brands across the globe.

Rooted in Ayurvedic wisdom and driven by modern science, we've spent two decades perfecting formulations, expanding capacity, and earning the certifications that let our partners sell with confidence — at home and in 45+ international markets.

  • Founder-led, values-driven organisation
  • In-house R&D, QC and production under one roof
  • Trusted by startups, enterprises and global exporters
